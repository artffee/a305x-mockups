A305X is the independent art world of Ivaylo Peytchev: flamingo designs, surreal humor, and a stubborn belief that creativity and kindness deserve more space than war and division.

Penthouse / Flamingo Briefing

Miami woke up to a bird that refused the default. White linework. Pink tongue. A message loud enough for Ocean Drive and quiet enough for a notebook page.

The flock never filed an application. Specimen 305 simply appeared: anti-war, pro-human, beautifully unmanageable. The mission fits on one line — Question the default. Keep your humor. Make peace visible.
